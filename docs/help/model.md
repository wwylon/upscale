# Model

On the **Scale** tab choose **Neural model** or **Classical** (Lanczos, paper algorithms, or vectorize). Built-in neural models cover photos, anime, denoising, and fast SPAN.

Open **Model library** (header **Models** link, Method button, or Settings) to rename, edit descriptions, hide models from lists (weights stay on disk), download missing weights, or add external weights by URL, file, or path (prefer `.safetensors`). Denoise strength applies to the wdn model. Models that are not downloaded or failed to load are marked; the header shows how many need attention.
